# UI audit of every workspace (2026-11-01)

Audited on the story-demo stack (release `669e1c2b…`, September replay at speed 20) in
light and dark themes, with full-page captures of all ten workspaces, an automated scan
of every legend, and independent ClickHouse queries for the headline numbers.

## Problems found

Each problem names the pull request that fixes it.

### Legends and colour (PR: legend and colour correctness)

1. Insights · loyalty: "No CRM tier" (32.1 % of net revenue) uses the muted surface colour; its
   donut slice and legend mark are invisible on the card in both themes.
2. Insights · loyalty: Gold (amber) and Bronze (orange) cannot be told apart (ΔE 2.2 protan,
   7.9 normal vision).
3. Insights · web vs app and Products · devices: blue against violet is below the
   normal-vision floor (ΔE 14.2), so Web/App and Mobile/Desktop look alike.
4. Products · treemap: categories were validated only as neighbours in legend order, but a
   treemap puts any two categories side by side; Electronics (blue) touches Sports (violet).
5. Email: the same measure changes colour between the two charts (Opened is blue in the
   monthly lines and violet in the funnel; Clicked is orange, then green), and the legend
   lists Clicked, Opened, Sent instead of the funnel order.
6. Live View: blue means "App" in the web/app split and "an order" in the shop floor and the
   minute chart; the minute chart explains its marks in a sentence instead of a legend.

### Numbers and wording (PR: readable numbers and labels)

7. Compact money always rounds down: ₫3,457,576,000 reads ₫3.45B, ₫668,639,347 reads ₫668.63M.
8. Journey analysis prints raw ratios ("0.848271 of sessions") and six-decimal allocated
   orders ("1.000000").
9. Raw technical times: ISO timestamps in journeys and in "Last successful check", and
   "last event received 35,805s ago".
10. Channels appear lowercase ("tiktok", "google") on Journey analysis while every other page
    says TikTok and Google.
11. Monospace currency figures render the ₫ sign as a raised, oversized glyph.
12. Data health shows "Unavailable: lineage", an internal key, as its error.

### When customers act (PR: Vietnam time on Insights)

13. The weekday-hour heatmap and its busiest slot are only in UTC ("Monday 02:00–03:00 UTC"),
    while marketers plan in Vietnam time (UTC+7; no daylight saving).

### Customers (PR: readable customer workspace)

14. Cohort retention lists CRM cohorts from 2023 first; their activity starts at month 13, so
    the first screen is a wall of "Unavailable" cells and the grid is 44 columns wide.
15. The profile table shows internal codes (`unidentified_profile`, `marketing_denied`,
    `high_value`), money without currency, an unformatted total ("28544 matching"), and its
    last column is clipped.

### Ad performance (PR: campaign scatter guides)

16. Scatter bubbles are unlabelled: a marketer must hover each one to learn the campaign,
    and nothing marks which campaigns are above or below the typical spend and return.
17. The spend-share card leaves half of its height empty next to the efficiency card.

### Geography (PR: legible revenue map)

18. The map is drawn at about 240 px wide with 9 px labels; Ha Noi/Hai Phong and
    Can Tho/Ho Chi Minh City overlap, and the bubble size has no scale.

### Date ranges (PR: shared date presets)

19. Each page opens on a different window (Overview 90 days, Ads the whole history, Journey
    analysis one year) and only Overview offers one-click ranges.

## Checked and fine

Overview KPIs and trend, channel donut, stage reach; Ads efficiency small multiples; Products
top products; Geography city ranking; Live activity provisional bars; Data health meter and
labelled states; light and dark both free of horizontal overflow at 1440 px.
